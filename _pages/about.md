I am a Professor of Mathematics (classe exceptionnelle) at [Université Paris Cité](https://u-paris.fr/) and a member of the Mathematical Logic team of the [Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche (IMJ-PRG)](https://www.imj-prg.fr/), UMR 7586.

My research is in **set theory** and its applications: forcing axioms and iterated forcing (side conditions, virtual models), large cardinals, infinitary logics and Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé games, and applications of set theory to topology, algebra and operator algebras. See [Research](/research/) for more.

I obtained my Ph.D. at the University of Wisconsin–Madison under the direction of Kenneth Kunen, was a postdoc at Caltech and at UC Berkeley, and spent time at the University of Toronto and Carnegie Mellon University before joining Université Paris Diderot (now Université Paris Cité). My [mathematical genealogy](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=10233) lists my doctoral students.

I am co-director of the Master 2 [Logique Mathématique et Fondements de l'Informatique (LMFI)](http://www.math.univ-paris-diderot.fr/m2lmfi/) and of the Master [LOGOS](https://master-logos.fr/). I am a founding member and past President (2018–2021) of the [European Set Theory Society](https://ests.wordpress.com/).
