My research is in mathematical logic, more precisely in set theory. Set theory plays a special role in logic: on one hand it deals with foundational questions, such as the choice of axioms and the consistency of mathematical theories; on the other hand its techniques have important applications in other areas of mathematics, such as Banach spaces, operator algebras and homological algebra. I have worked on both the *pure* and the *applied* side of the subject.

Forcing axioms and iterated forcing
------
Forcing axioms have been a central theme of my work since my thesis, which showed that Martin's Axiom MA<sub>ℵ<sub>1</sub></sub> is equivalent to a topological statement studied by Shanin in the 1930s. More recently I have worked on Neeman's method of iterating forcing with finite chains of elementary submodels as side conditions. I introduced *virtual models*, which make it possible to iterate larger classes of forcing notions, such as semi-proper forcing, and to obtain the consistency of generalized forcing axioms (for instance, principles that hold simultaneously at ω<sub>2</sub> and ω<sub>3</sub>).

Infinitary logic and Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé games
------
With Jouko Väänänen I study Shelah's logic 𝕃<sub>κ</sub><sup>1</sup>, the first infinitary logic admitting a Lindström-type characterization, and families of logics generalizing it, defined via Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé games. A current project, *Infinitary logic and forcing*, also aims to use infinitary logic (in the spirit of Jensen's 𝓛-forcing) to build new forcing notions, and to iterate them using side conditions.

Applications of set theory
------
* **Algebraic topology and homological algebra:** with A. Vignati, the consistency of non-vanishing higher derived limits lim<sup>n</sup> **A** for all n > 0, answering a question going back to Mardešić and Prasolov.
* **Rigidity:** applications of the Open Colouring Axiom to automorphisms of quotient structures, from 𝒫(ω)/fin to reduced powers 𝒜<sup>ℕ</sup>/Fin.
* **Operator algebras:** with B. De Bondt, A. Vaccaro and A. Vignati, applications of the infinitary logic 𝓛<sub>ω<sub>1</sub>ω</sub> to AF-algebras.
* Earlier work includes descriptive set theory and classification problems (with A. Louveau, S. Shelah and S. Thomas), as well as computational complexity and derandomization (with M. Luby, A. Wigderson and others).
